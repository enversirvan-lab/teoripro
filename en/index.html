About the Swedish Theory Test

To obtain a driving license in Sweden, you must pass the Swedish Transport Agency's theory test, which consists of a total of 65 questions and requires at least 52 correct answers to pass.

Our website, with a question bank of real exam quality, allows you to prepare for the Swedish driving test in a practical and efficient way.

Swedish standard: Adapted to the official exam format and terminology.
Instant results: Once you complete the test, you will immediately see your mistakes and correct explanations.

What should you consider when preparing for the driving test?

The theory test during the driving license process measures how well driver candidates understand traffic rules, engine knowledge, first aid, and traffic ethics. The interactive tests on our website are specially designed to reduce exam stress, refresh knowledge, and facilitate adaptation to the real exam format in a practical way.

What is the secret to success in the theory test?

Instead of just memorizing questions, understanding the logic behind traffic signs and rules, especially in visual and video-based questions, leads to passing the theory test on the first try and lays the foundation for safe driving both during the driving test and in the future. With our dynamic question pool, you can test yourself at any time and quickly identify your weak areas.

How does our exam system work?

All our tests are completely free and we avoid interruptions to keep the user experience at its best. No prerequisites are required to start the test; you can directly begin with your tests.

How can you evaluate your test results?

After each test, you will see your correct and incorrect answers in detail, and you can review explanations for your mistakes to identify your weaknesses. This way, you clearly see which subjects you need to revise.

Simulation closest to the real exam experience

Our exam simulation is designed according to the Swedish Transport Agency's official exam format. With 65 questions, 60 minutes, and a requirement of at least 52 correct answers, you can experience the real exam atmosphere. With the random question option, you face different questions each time and can prepare for all topics that may appear on the test.
